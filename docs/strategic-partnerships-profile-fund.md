# Strategic Partnerships, Profile & External Engagement Fund

**Accounting home:** 2.4.4 Graduate Premium Adoption & Activation  
**Budget control:** King’s Edge programme leadership  
**Deployment:** may support activity across the King’s Edge portfolio where it advances strategic partnerships, external profile, relationship development or purposeful external engagement.

## Planning envelope

- 2026/27: £25,000
- 2027/28: £35,000
- 2028/29: £45,000
- BAU from 2029/30: £45,000 p.a.

The fund is additional to the existing 2.4.4 activation, research and survey-intervention envelopes. It may support targeted UK and international mobility, partner development and reciprocal visits, employer/alumni/civic/international relationship-building, strategic network participation, convening, hosting, dissemination and small exploratory partnership activity.

It is not a general travel or conference budget. Deployment should have a clear relationship to King’s Edge partnership development, external profile, strategic learning or programme priorities.
