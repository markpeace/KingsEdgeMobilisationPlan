## Shared mobilisation pattern for purpose, skills and recognition

Deliverables 2.2.1, 2.1.3 and 2.4.3 share a deliberate mobilisation pattern:

1. **Discover** the educational, technical, policy and user questions before fixing the institutional solution.
2. **Trailblaze and build capability** with a small number of willing programmes, departments, faculties or student-facing partners prepared to go sufficiently deep to test the proposition properly.
3. **Test portability** so that success dependent on exceptional local enthusiasm is not mistaken for an institutionally scalable model.
4. **Institutionalise what works** through mainstream curriculum, policy, operating and digital routes, preserving justified local variation.

This is intentionally deeper than a lightweight pilot model. The purpose of trailblazers is to build real local capability and expose the practical conditions for success before King’s codifies a common approach.
